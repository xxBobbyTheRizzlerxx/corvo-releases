# deskbar

A floating top bar for Windows with your clock, music, system stats, calendar and recent apps, styled your way.

## Download

**[Download deskbar for Windows](https://github.com/xxBobbyTheRizzlerxx/deskbar-releases/releases/latest/download/deskbar-setup.exe)** (Windows 10 and 11)

Run `deskbar-setup.exe` and follow the steps. deskbar isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

After that deskbar keeps itself up to date.
